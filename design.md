# Atlas Assessment — Design Brief for Stitch

> **Product**: Atlas Assessment
> **Brand (hero)**: Seriously Addictive Mathematics (S.A.M.)
> **Sub-brand**: "Powered by Inspirea Labs"
> **Purpose**: This document specifies the UI/UX for an interactive prototype to be generated by Stitch. It is screen-by-screen and opinionated. Every screen should be designable from the spec below without further questions.

## 1. Brand & Visual System

### Brand Hierarchy
- **Hero brand**: S.A.M. (Seriously Addictive Mathematics) — the wordmark with the recognizable period accents. Use the official S.A.M. logo lockup wherever a logo appears.
- **Sub-line**: "Powered by Inspirea Labs" — small, secondary text, placed under the S.A.M. logo on splash/login screens, in the footer of every page, and on the parent/instructor report header.
- **Product name**: "Atlas Assessment" — appears as a section/product label, never larger than the S.A.M. logo.

### Typography

|-------|--------|----------|
| `display-child` | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | Child-facing display / headlines |
| `headline-adult` | **Inter** | Parent / instructor headlines |
| `body-regular` | **Inter** | Body copy across all surfaces |
| `math-numeral` | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | Numerals in math questions |

Both families are open-source via Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans: SIL Open Font License; Inter: SIL Open Font License). Self-host via `next/font/google` for performance and to avoid third-party requests on child screens.

### Visual Mood
**Bright and playful** — think Duolingo and Khan Academy Kids. Rounded corners (12–24px), generous white space, soft shadows, warm illustrations, gentle animations. Never sterile. Never dark.

### Iconography
- Rounded, friendly icons (Lucide React with rounded stroke style, or Phosphor "duotone" style).
- For child-facing screens: chunky, colorful, expressive.
- For parent/instructor screens: cleaner, more restrained, but still rounded.

## 3. Mascot System

### K-4 Mascot: "Long haired mini dachshund"
- **Character**: A friendly dachshund — chosen because dachshunds are clever, playful, and smart. Approachable to young children, neutral across cultures.
- **Personality**: Playful peer — silly, enthusiastic, kid-energy. Says things like "Whoa, look at this one!" and "Ooh, that was tricky! Let's try another." Never condescending, never preachy.
- **Visual style**: Per sample screens. Multiple poses: greeting/waving, thinking (paw on chin), cheering, encouraging.
- **Role**: Mascot appears on the welcome screen, between question groups (every ~5 questions) for a quick word of encouragement, and on the completion screen. Never appears during a question itself — that screen stays focused on the math.

### 5-8 Experience: Journey Map
- No mascot. Older kids find it patronizing.
- Replace with a **journey visual**: a winding path or map with milestone markers. The child's avatar (a customizable chip — star, planet, rocket, etc.) moves along the path as they progress. Each milestone is a question group.
- Aesthetic: think a stripped-down, more mature version of the Duolingo path. Calm, rewarding, no characters.

## 4. Screen Inventory

The full flow has four user types. The final number of screens are per Stitch design. Basic screens are listed below, Stitch may produce more variants.

### Parent Flow (Desktop)
1. **Landing / Splash** — S.A.M. branding, value prop, "Get Started" CTA
2. **Parent Signup** — email + password, COPPA consent checkbox
3. **Email Verification** — "Check your email" interstitial
4. **Add Child** — name, birth year, grade level (optional)
5. **Parent Dashboard** — list of children, "Start Assessment" CTA per child, history of past assessments
6. **Assessment Handoff** — "Hand the tablet to [child's name] and tap Start" screen, designed to be shown on tablet
7. **Results Report (Parent View)** — full diagnostic report (see §6)
8. **Re-assess Confirmation Modal** — "Ready to take another assessment? Best to wait at least 6–8 weeks."

### Child K-4 Flow (Tablet)
9. **Welcome (with Sammy)** — "Hi, I'm Sammy! What's your name?" with name confirmation
10. **Practice Question** — one ungraded sample to teach the interaction
11. **Question Screen (MC)** — multiple choice with large tappable tiles
12. **Question Screen (Numeric)** — number pad entry
13. **Question Screen (Drag-Drop)** — visual manipulation (e.g., bar model)
14. **Mascot Encouragement Interstitial** — Sammy says something between question groups
15. **Completion Screen** — celebration, "Great job! Show your grown-up." instruction

### Child 5-8 Flow (Tablet)
Reuses screens 11–13 (question screens) with these variants:
16. **Welcome (Journey Map)** — name confirmation, avatar selection, "Begin your journey"
17. **Journey Map Interstitial** — shown between question groups; avatar advances along the path
18. **Completion Screen (5-8 variant)** — "Journey complete!" with stats summary

### Instructor Flow (Desktop)
19. **Instructor Login** — separate entry point from parent signup
20. **Student Roster** — table of students assigned to the instructor, with status (assessment complete, in progress, not started)
21. **Individual Student Report** — same diagnostic report as parents see, with additional pedagogical notes section
22. **Cohort View** — aggregated stats across the instructor's students (placement distribution, common misconceptions)

## 5. Detailed Screen Specs

### Screen 1: Landing / Splash (Desktop)
**Layout**: Single-column hero, centered content, max-width 1200px.
- **Top bar**: S.A.M. logo (left, ~160px wide), "Powered by Inspirea Labs" small text underneath. Login link (top right).
- **Hero section** (full viewport):
  - Headline: "Find out exactly where your child stands in math — in 15 minutes, from your couch."
  - Subhead: "An AI-powered assessment from S.A.M., the world's largest Singapore Math enrichment program."
  - CTA button: "Start Free Assessment" (large, `sam-red`, rounded 16px)
  - Right side: Friendly illustration — Sammy the Otter holding a tablet, with floating math icons (numbers, shapes) around.
- **Three-up benefits row** below the fold:
  - Icon + headline + 1-line description, ×3
  - "🎯 Adaptive — adjusts to your child"
  - "🧠 Diagnostic — sees how they think"
  - "📊 Actionable — gives you a plan"
- **Footer**: S.A.M. logo, "Powered by Inspirea Labs", links (Privacy, Terms, Contact)

### Screen 2: Parent Signup (Desktop)
**Layout**: Two-column. Left = form. Right = trust signals (S.A.M. logo, "20+ countries, 200+ centers, 15 years of research").
- Form fields: Email, Password (with strength meter), Confirm Password
- COPPA consent checkbox: "I am the parent or legal guardian of the child(ren) I will register, and I consent to the collection of their assessment data as described in our Privacy Policy."
- CTA: "Create Account" (full-width, `sam-red`)
- Below: "Already have an account? Log in"

### Screen 4: Add Child (Desktop)
**Layout**: Centered card on cream background.
- Card title: "Tell us about your child"
- Fields:
  - Child's first name
  - Birth year (dropdown, current year - 4 to current year - 14)
  - Current grade in school (optional dropdown: Pre-K, K, 1st, 2nd, ... 8th)
- CTA: "Continue" (`sam-red`)
- Below: "Add another child later from your dashboard."

### Screen 5: Parent Dashboard (Desktop)
**Layout**: Sidebar nav (left, 240px) + main content (right).
- **Sidebar**: S.A.M. logo top, "Powered by Inspirea Labs" subtext, nav items (Children, Reports, Settings, Logout)
- **Main**:
  - Header: "Hi, [Parent Name]"
  - Child cards (one per child), each containing:
    - Child name, age, grade
    - Last assessment date + placement level (e.g., "Last assessed: Mar 2026 — Placed at Level 2B")
    - "View Report" button (secondary)
    - "Start New Assessment" button (`sam-red` primary)
  - "+ Add Another Child" button (outlined)

### Screen 6: Assessment Handoff (Tablet)
**Layout**: Full-screen, centered, designed for the moment the parent hands the iPad to the child.
- S.A.M. logo top
- Sammy waving illustration, large
- Headline: "Ready, [Child Name]?"
- Subhead: "Hand the tablet to your child and tap Start."
- Big button: "Start" (large, `sam-red`, full-width on tablet)
- Small text below: "Tip: Find a quiet spot. Headphones welcome. No pressure!"

### Screen 9: Welcome with Sammy (Tablet, K-4)
**Layout**: Full-screen, playful.
- Background: Soft `sam-cream` with subtle floating shapes
- Sammy the Otter, large (40% of screen), waving
- Speech bubble from Sammy: "Hi! I'm Sammy! What should I call you?"
- Input field (large, friendly): pre-filled with the name parent entered, child can confirm or edit
- Big button: "That's me!" (`sam-red`, large)

### Screen 11: Question Screen — Multiple Choice (Tablet)
**Layout**: Clean, focused, no distractions.
- **Top bar** (slim, 60px): S.A.M. logo small (top left), progress indicator (subtle journey map or dot trail — never "Question 7 of 25"), pause button (top right, small, gray)
- **Question area** (top 40% of screen):
  - Question text in large, friendly font (Fredoka, ~36pt)
  - Sample question: "What is 47 - 19?"
  - If applicable, an illustration or visual aid (e.g., a number line, a bar model)
- **Answer area** (bottom 60% of screen):
  - For MC: 4 large tappable tiles in a 2×2 grid
  - Each tile: rounded (24px corners), `white` bg, thick border (`sam-gray-light`), large numeral inside
  - On tap: tile briefly bounces (Framer Motion), border becomes `sam-red`, then proceeds to next question
  - Sample answers for "47 - 19": `28`, `38`, `26`, `32`
- **No "Submit" button** — tapping an answer auto-advances. (Reduces hesitation, keeps flow.)
- **No correct/incorrect feedback** — child never knows if they got it right.

### Screen 12: Question Screen — Numeric Entry (Tablet)
- Same top bar and question area as Screen 11
- **Answer area**: Large input field at top of bottom section, with a custom large-button number pad below (0-9, backspace, "Done" button in `sam-red`)
- Sample question: "Maya has 24 stickers. She gives 8 to her brother. How many does she have left?"

### Screen 13: Question Screen — Drag and Drop (Tablet)
- Same top bar
- Question area shows the visual prompt: "Drag the numbers to make the bar model match: 12 + ? = 30"
- Bottom area: a visual bar model with a missing piece. Draggable number tiles (e.g., `15`, `18`, `20`, `22`) sit at the bottom; child drags one into the empty bar segment.
- On drop, the tile snaps in, brief celebration animation (gentle bounce, no confetti — save that for completion), auto-advances after 800ms.

### Screen 14: Mascot Encouragement Interstitial (Tablet, K-4)
**Layout**: Brief, full-screen, playful.
- Sammy in different pose (cheering, thumbs up, etc.)
- Speech bubble: rotates between phrases like:
  - "Whoa, you're flying through these!"
  - "Some of these are tricky — keep going!"
  - "I love how hard you're thinking!"
- Auto-advances after 2.5 seconds, or child can tap "Keep going" button

### Screen 17: Journey Map Interstitial (Tablet, 5-8)
**Layout**: Full-screen, calm, rewarding.
- Background: subtle landscape illustration (mountains, paths, stars)
- The child's avatar (chip selected at start) moves along a winding path
- Milestone markers along the path light up as they're passed
- Brief stat at bottom: "You've completed 8 questions" — no pass/fail signal
- Auto-advances after 2 seconds, or child taps to continue

### Screen 15/18: Completion Screen (Tablet)
**Layout**: Celebratory, brief, hands the experience back to the parent.
- **K-4 version**: Sammy doing a happy dance, confetti animation (subtle), "All done! Amazing work!" headline, "Show this screen to your grown-up." subtext
- **5-8 version**: Avatar at the end of the journey path with a fluttering flag or trophy, "Journey complete!" headline, "Bring this to your parent or instructor."
- Both versions: Large "Show My Grown-up" button that, when tapped, displays a parent-facing confirmation: "Assessment complete. Results are ready in your dashboard."
- No score shown to child. Ever.

### Screen 7: Results Report — Parent View (Desktop)
This is the moneyshot. Designed to feel like a premium diagnostic report — the kind a parent screenshots and shares with their spouse or grandparent.

**Layout**: Single column, max-width 880px, centered. Print-friendly.

- **Header bar**:
  - S.A.M. logo (left)
  - "Powered by Inspirea Labs" small subtext under logo
  - "Download as PDF" button (top right)
- **Report header card** (`sam-cream` bg, rounded):
  - Child's name (large, `sam-navy`)
  - Assessment date
  - Big placement statement: "Maya is ready for Dimensions Math **Level 2B**." (level number in `sam-red`, oversized)
- **Strand Map section**:
  - Section title: "How Maya Thinks About Math"
  - Horizontal bar chart, one row per strand:
    - Number Sense — bar filled to "Level 3A"
    - Operations — bar filled to "Level 3B"
    - Word Problems — bar filled to "Level 2A" (visibly shorter — draws the eye)
    - Fractions & Decimals — "Level 2B"
    - Geometry — "Level 2B"
    - Measurement & Data — "Level 3A"
  - Each bar uses the strand's accent color, with a subtle gradient
  - Brief 1-line caption under the chart: "Your child is strongest in computation and benefits from extra word-problem practice."
- **Misconception Insights section**:
  - Section title: "What We Noticed"
  - 2–4 cards, each containing:
    - Icon (e.g., 🔢 for number, 📖 for reading the problem)
    - Headline: "Hasn't fully internalized regrouping in subtraction"
    - 2-sentence plain-language explanation
    - Sample: "When solving 47 - 19, Maya answered 38 — subtracting the smaller digit from the larger one in the ones column instead of regrouping. This is one of the most common subtraction patterns at this level and is straightforward to address."
- **Recommendations section**:
  - Section title: "What To Do Next"
  - Numbered list (3–5 items), each with bold action + 1-line context:
    1. **Start with Dimensions Math 2B** — Maya is ready for the conceptual content at this level.
    2. **Supplement Chapter 5 (Subtraction) with Extra Practice 2A** — to solidify regrouping before moving forward.
    3. **Add Challenging Word Problems 2** — to build the analytical muscle that lags behind her computation.
    4. **Re-assess in 8–10 weeks** to confirm progress and adjust the plan.
- **Footer card**: S.A.M. branding, contact info, "Schedule a free consultation with a S.A.M. instructor" CTA (secondary)

### Screen 20: Instructor Student Roster (Desktop)
**Layout**: Sidebar nav + main table.
- **Sidebar**: S.A.M. logo + "Instructor Portal" label, "Powered by Inspirea Labs" subtext, nav (Students, Cohorts, Settings, Logout)
- **Main**:
  - Header: "Your Students" + filter/search bar
  - Table columns: Student Name | Grade | Last Assessment | Placement Level | Status (Complete / In Progress / Not Started) | Action (View Report)
  - Sortable, filterable, paginated
  - "Invite Student" button top right

### Screen 21: Instructor Individual Report (Desktop)
- Identical to the Parent Results Report (Screen 7)
- **Plus** an additional section at the bottom: "Pedagogical Notes" — instructor-only commentary:
  - Suggested first lesson focus
  - Recommended worksheets from the S.A.M. system
  - Notes on engagement style observed (e.g., "Took longer on visual problems — may benefit from more hands-on materials")
- And an "Add Note" function for the instructor to record their own observations after the child's first session

### Screen 22: Cohort View (Desktop)
**Layout**: Dashboard with charts.
- Top: Summary stat tiles — Total Students, Average Placement Level, Most Common Misconception, Average Time to Complete
- Mid: Placement distribution chart (bar chart showing how many students at each level)
- Bottom: Top 5 misconceptions across the cohort (horizontal bar list with frequency)
- Right sidebar: List of students needing attention (e.g., placement well below grade level, or unusually long completion times)

## 7. Interaction & Animation Principles

- **No "submit" buttons during the assessment** — tapping an answer auto-advances.
- **No correct/incorrect feedback during the assessment** — preserves the diagnostic.
- **All transitions are gentle** — 200–400ms, ease-in-out. Never abrupt.
- **Sammy moves and speaks** — simple sprite-style animations between questions, never during.
- **Celebration is rare and earned** — only on completion, never per-question. (Avoids reinforcing right/wrong.)
- **Loading states are friendly** — Sammy thinking, or a soft pulsing "Getting your next question…" with the journey path animating.

## 8. Sample Question Bank (For Realistic Mockups)

Stitch should use these exact questions in mockup screens to make the prototype feel real.

### Multiple Choice (Grade 2)

### Visual / Geometry (Grade 4)
- "Which shape has all four sides equal?" — visual choice between square, rectangle, parallelogram, trapezoid
- "What is the perimeter of this rectangle?" — show a 5×3 rectangle

## 9. Accessibility Requirements

- **Touch targets**: Minimum 44×44px (Apple HIG). For K-4 child screens, minimum 88×88px.
- **Color contrast**: WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text). Test all combinations against `sam-cream` background.
- **No color-only signaling**: Never use color alone to indicate state (always pair with icon, label, or position).
- **Audio alternatives**: Question text should be readable by screen readers. (Optional: TTS audio playback for younger children — flag for future enhancement, not v1.)
- **Keyboard navigation**: Parent and instructor desktop screens must be fully keyboard-navigable. Child tablet screens are touch-only by design.
