Our Commitment

The Seriously Addictive Mathematics (S.A.M.) platform is committed to protecting the privacy of children. The Atlas Assessment is designed to be a safe space for mathematical discovery.

Information We Collect

  • check_circle

    Assessment Data: Numerical answers and problem-solving patterns.

  • check_circle

    Progress Metrics: Time taken per question and completion status.

  • check_circle

    User Identity: First name and grade level only (no full identities).

Parental Rights

Under COPPA, parents have the right to review their child's information, request its deletion, and refuse further collection. S.A.M. does not share child data with third-party advertisers.
